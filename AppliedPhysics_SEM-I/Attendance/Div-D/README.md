The attendance record is maintained primarily to help students **monitor their attendance regularly**. Students are encouraged to report any discrepancy as soon as possible.

### For Attendance-related Queries

Students should contact the **Class In-charge or the concerned subject faculty** with the following information:

* Student Name
* Roll Number
* Date of absence
* Subject/lecture concerned
* Reason for absence, if applicable
* Supporting document, where required

**Please avoid sending repeated messages regarding the same attendance entry.** Allow reasonable time for verification and updating of the record.

## Instructions for Students

1. **Roll numbers listed against a date indicate students who were absent** on that particular day.
2. Students are expected to attend all lectures, practicals, tutorials, and other academic activities as per the college timetable.
3. Students who are absent due to a **valid reason** should inform the Class In-charge/concerned faculty at the earliest.
4. In case of medical, family, or other unavoidable circumstances, students may be required to submit appropriate supporting documents.
5. If a student believes that their attendance has been recorded incorrectly, they should contact the concerned faculty **at the earliest** rather than waiting until the end of the semester.
6. Students are responsible for keeping track of their attendance and ensuring that they meet the **minimum attendance requirement prescribed by the University/College**.
7. Attendance records may be updated or corrected if any genuine discrepancy is identified.
8. Students should regularly check this page for attendance updates.
9. **Being absent from the attendance record does not automatically imply that the absence is approved.** Approval of leave will be subject to the applicable college rules.
10. Students are advised to avoid unnecessary absence, particularly during the initial/foundation lectures, as missing early lectures may make it difficult to catch up with the course.

 **Note:** The attendance record will be maintained and updated periodically by the Class In-charge/concerned faculty.
